# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

## What we collect

We use no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking technology of any kind on this website.

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, message and organization if you provide one. The form sends this information to our email service to deliver your inquiry. You can also email **contact@1kb.software** directly.

We process this information solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss the services you request, based on our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries or taking steps at your request before entering a contract.

## How we use it

Your data is used exclusively to handle your inquiry. We do not sell or rent it. Hosting and email providers may process it on our behalf as needed to deliver and respond to your message.

## How long we keep it

Contact messages are retained for up to 12 months, then permanently deleted.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** any personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Request deletion** of your data at any time
- **Object** to processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email **contact@1kb.software**. We will respond within 30 days.
